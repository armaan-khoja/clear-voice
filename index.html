Clear Voice

Whisper fine-tuned for people with dysarthria, tested on people and sentences it never heard. Armaan Khoja and Suvir Aylawadi, Windermere Preparatory School.

Code and results on GitHub Research paper (PDF) Download the video (MP4)
Recordings from the TORGO database (Rudzicz, Namasivayam and Wolff, 2012), used for non-commercial research. Clear Voice is a research prototype, not a medical device.
